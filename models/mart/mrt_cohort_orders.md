{% docs mrt_cohort_orders %}

This model generates a cohort report to follow the rntention of customers after a first order.
It includes the following metrics grouped by cohort :
- **Month_offset to see the time passed after the first order**
- **Revenue generated by orders.** 
- **Number of customers who pursuied their first order (A).**
- **Number of customers who pursuied another order (B).**
- **Customers retention rate calculated by doing B/A**

This allows for analysis of the retention of customers after their first order
and the delay passed between the first order and the next
