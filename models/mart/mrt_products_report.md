{% docs mrt_products_report %}

This model generates a report with key insights on products, including the following metrics:
- **Total volume of sales by product.**
- **Total revenue generated by product.** 
- **Total of discount on the product.**
- **Stock of product.**

This allows for analysis by comparaison with the metrics on the company level
- **Total revenue of the entreprise.**
- **Total volume of sales.**
